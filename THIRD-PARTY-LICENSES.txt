The LGPL libraries are shipped as separate DLL/PYD files in python\Lib\site-packages and can be replaced by the
user with compatible versions. Their source code is available at the addresses above.

The LGPL Qt libraries are separate files in python/lib/python3.13/site-packages/PySide6 and can be replaced likewise.

Speech models are NOT included. They are downloaded by the user from Hugging Face and keep their own licences:
OpenAI Whisper and its CTranslate2 conversions (MIT), Distil-Whisper (MIT), SenseVoiceSmall by FunAudioLLM
(FunASR Model Open Source License Agreement), Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v3 by NVIDIA (CC-BY-4.0), Moonshine by Useful
Sensors (MIT). The model manager shows each model's licence before it is downloaded.

AI summary models are NOT included either. They are downloaded by the user from Hugging Face: Qwen3-1.7B,
Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507, Qwen3.5-2B and Qwen3.5-4B by the Qwen team, Alibaba Cloud, and Gemma 4 E2B and E4B by
Google (all Apache-2.0; GGUF files as published by Qwen or converted by Unsloth).

Speaker identification models (version 2.4.0) are NOT included either. They are downloaded by the user from
Hugging Face: pyannote segmentation 3.0 by CNRS / pyannote (MIT; ONNX conversion by the sherpa-onnx project,
huggingface.co/csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-pyannote-segmentation-3-0) and the 3D-Speaker CAM++ voice models by the
Speech Lab of Alibaba's Institute for Intelligent Computing (Apache-2.0; ONNX files from
huggingface.co/csukuangfj/speaker-embedding-models). They run with sherpa-onnx, which is included (Apache-2.0).

Screenshots on the project page use a sample from the LibriSpeech ASR corpus (V. Panayotov et al., CC BY 4.0).
