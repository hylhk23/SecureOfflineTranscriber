The LGPL libraries are shipped as separate DLL/PYD files in python\Lib\site-packages and can be replaced by the
user with compatible versions. Their source code is available at the addresses above.

Speech models are NOT included. They are downloaded by the user from Hugging Face and keep their own licences:
OpenAI Whisper and its CTranslate2 conversions (MIT), Distil-Whisper (MIT), SenseVoiceSmall by FunAudioLLM
(FunASR Model Open Source License Agreement), Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v3 by NVIDIA (CC-BY-4.0), Moonshine by Useful
Sensors (MIT). The model manager shows each model's licence before it is downloaded.

Screenshots on the project page use a sample from the LibriSpeech ASR corpus (V. Panayotov et al., CC BY 4.0).
